# Build 9E — Staff Workdays and Compensation

Baseline: bb5d30e0ffc7e847c7275a2656a9468094d11108 (verified Build 9D, Render Live). Isolated branch build-9e-workdays, remote Codespace worktree /workspaces/btw-build-9e. No main merge, production, infrastructure, force-push or Build 9F changes.

## Discovery and reference
Django had StaffProfile, StaffAssignment and AssignmentDay, but no staff workday ledger, compensation configuration or driving-distance service. Assignment roles are descriptive staffing responsibilities, not pay roles. Participant Attendance is unrelated and remains untouched. Reviewed the existing Builds 9B–9D implementations and documentation; no separate Build 9A roadmap file was present in this checkout.

Authenticated Base44 editor was inspected read-only: event Clock lists attendance and work reports by date; manager Add Work Report selects employee, event, work date and daily role. Payroll Settings shows Driver 400/day, Staff 200/day, Staff Lead 250/day, Combined 600/day; global per diem is 40/day with eligibility enabled on these four roles. These verified daily settings seed the new Django configuration effective 2026-10-19. Earlier rates are not inferred. Newly added roles default to eligibility review and no pay rate.

Base44 mileage uses fixed tiers. The user explicitly requested a true per-mile rate instead. No numeric mileage rate was supplied or invented; the initial mileage rate remains unconfigured. The administrator must enter an approved USD-per-mile rate with its effective date. No fixed tiers, paid mapping API, straight-line estimate or automatic reimbursement is introduced.

## Data model and staff workflow
New workdays app reuses Event, StaffProfile, AssignmentDay, Django auth and audit_action/AuditLog. StaffRole and append-only CompensationRate versions configure the daily-pay basis, role eligibility, global per diem and mileage rate. Workday stores role name, work date, driving declaration, source, original staff submission, effective check-in, creator/editor, compensation snapshots, approval state and active/void status. Existing role history remains valid after rename/deactivation.

Staff enter once on their confirmed AssignmentDay, on that local calendar date in the event timezone. Role and driving are independent; Driver alone creates no mileage claim. Staff cannot rewrite records, access another employee's compensation, change global rates or approve claims. A conditional database uniqueness constraint prevents duplicate active employee/event/date records. Missed-day and other historical corrections require managers; there is no clock-out, hourly calculation, GPS or narrative report.

Managers view the event-day roster and missing check-ins; add unassigned employees' workdays (including inactive profiles for historical corrections, without reactivating them); correct dates/roles/driving/effective local time; review per diem exceptions; approve, reopen and void. Manager Entry never acquires a false staff submission timestamp. Corrections retain original source/submission and create before/after audits. Approved workdays must be reopened before correction. Approval retains already reviewed financial snapshots; a correction is required to refresh missing or changed configuration. Voids retain history and leave active totals. Workday changes do not alter staffing assignments.

## Individual mileage and financial integrity
EventMileage records verified one-way driving miles from Watkinsville, Georgia to the event physical address, confirmer and reason. Managers enter and confirm distance; no mapping provider or guessed distance is used. Venue changes require fresh verification before claim approval.

MileageTrip is independent of daily check-ins, with employee/event/trip number, fixed origin, destination/rate/distance/amount snapshots, travel period, source, reviewer and audit history. Repeated driving declarations link to an existing event trip rather than multiply reimbursement. A manager must explicitly confirm an additional complete round trip. Multiple employees can drive independently. Manager-entered declarations retain manager attribution. Voided trip history is not silently recreated as another payable trip; managers explicitly record legitimate additional/replacement travel.

Managers first verify the claim, review the calculated snapshot, then approve. Calculation is one-way driving miles × 2 × approved effective per-mile rate, rounded half-up to cents. Approval preserves the verified snapshot even if global rates or route distance later change. Missing rate/distance prevents verification/approval; pending declarations remain visible. Managers cannot approve their own mileage. Reopening enables audited correction; voids remain recorded and are excluded from totals. A workday void does not silently void an independent travel claim; managers review those separately.

Writes use existing staff guards, dedicated manage_workdays (also requiring events.view_event) and manage_compensation permissions, row locks, CSRF POST forms and revision checks. Database constraints enforce active-day/trip uniqueness, nonnegative rates/distances/amounts, source attribution, doubled round-trip miles and required verified/approved financial snapshots. No migration automatically grants new permissions. Authorized administrators grant them through existing auth administration; managers do not automatically receive compensation-settings access.

## Portal and reporting
Operations exposes My Workday History, Staff Workdays & Mileage and reports. My Schedule and the assigned event expose Daily Check-In & Workday History. Event Detail/Staffing links to workday management; Staff Profile links to filtered workday/correction/mileage history. Admin exposes functional Staff Compensation Settings for roles, pay-rate versions, eligibility, per diem and mileage. Six global sidebar sections and personal Settings gear are retained; no redesign.

Reports filter event, employee, date range and role. Workday and trip rows are separate in both portal and CSV. Trip date is known outbound date, otherwise event start date. A role filter includes each matching employee/event trip once. Approved totals include only active approved workdays and approved trips; void/pending/reopened records do not count. Missing configuration stays unconfigured/blank rather than becoming a payable zero. CSV escapes spreadsheet formula prefixes, is permission-protected, private/no-store and audited. Pagination preserves filters.

## Validation and scope
Focused acceptance suite covers staff permissions and timezone dates, duplicates, multi-day declarations, corrections, source/audits, multiple drivers, additional trips, approvals, historical snapshots, missing/invalid configuration, admin forms and dated rates, report totals/filters/pagination/CSV, CSRF and database constraints. Final executed counts and deployed commit are recorded in the delivery report. A populated synthetic Build 9D database copy is upgraded and original Event/Staff/Operations/Audit rows are hash-checked. Browser review uses isolated synthetic staff/manager/admin accounts and desktop/mobile widths.

No payroll payments, withholding, bank/provider integrations, hourly timekeeping, participant attendance, paid mapping, event expense accounting, offline sync or future-build work. The Build 9D operational-upload storage boundary is unchanged. Build 9F may consume these approved snapshots and distinct trip rows; it must not repeat trip payments per workday. Stop after preview publication and verification.
