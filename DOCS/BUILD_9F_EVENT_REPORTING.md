# Build 9F — Event Expenses, Performance Reporting and Analytics

Baseline: verified Build 9E cb83021ec5176b08660054ee03fea2729308abfa, Live on the existing Render preview. Branch build-9f-event-reporting, isolated remote worktree /workspaces/btw-build-9f. No main/production/infrastructure changes or force-push. Stop after preview verification; no 9G.

## Discovery
Reviewed Event/lifecycle, staffing assignments, Workday/MileageTrip snapshots, Operations notes and audit/auth/UI patterns. No manual expense, gift-card count/amount or incentive ledger existed in the verified Django model baseline; no legacy financial rows require conversion. Existing Base44 reports/analytics were inspected read-only: payroll and categorized manual expenses, manual lead results/notes, finalized report status, event-type/date filters and aggregate spend/cost-per-lead. External Base44 financial/lead values are not imported. Django has authoritative EventRegistration, Attendance, official ExperienceSession/ExperienceActivity, QuizAttempt, SimulatorCapture, and existing experience overview semantics. Existing system-test/demo, void, replacement and identity rules are reused.

## Ledgers and accounting
ExpenseCategory supports add/rename/activate/deactivate and historical usage. ManualExpense stores the event, immutable historical category label, category reference, decimal amount, date, description, notes/reference, treatment, creator/editor, reason, active/void and unique request key. IncentiveLine stores arbitrary decimal unit values and whole-number actually distributed quantities; total is unit value times quantity. No denominations are predefined. Financial writes require reason, CSRF, permissions, atomic event locks, stale revisions and audit before/after values. UUID submission keys reject repeated submissions. Voids never delete records; voided entries are read-only.

Manual treatment is explicit: operating cost is included; incentive purchases are retained but excluded; staff compensation references are retained but excluded. The seeded Gift cards and incentives category requires purchase treatment. Record actual distributed value in incentive lines once, not again as manual operating expense. Purchases are not an additional expense when distributed. Other categories may be configured as incentive purchase categories. Treatment and historical category labels on existing expense records are stable after category changes. Descriptions alone cannot establish whether two independently entered records represent the same real-world cost; managers reconcile source records and use the explicit treatments.

Approved daily pay and per diem come directly from active approved Build 9E Workday snapshots. Approved distinct MileageTrip snapshots contribute once per trip, including multiple employees/additional trips. Pending/reopened/void sources do not contribute. No duplicate source expense rows or copied financial amounts are stored. General expense forms cannot edit Workday/MileageTrip amounts; source links use 9E correction workflows.

Total staff cost = approved daily pay + approved per diem + approved mileage.
Recorded event subtotal = staff cost + active included operating expenses + active distributed incentive value.

## Completeness
No-entry components remain unknown until an authorized manager explicitly reconciles manual, incentive and staff sources, including deliberate zeros. CostReview retains the reviewer/reason and a fingerprint of financial sources, confirmed assignment days and event revision. Any changed source invalidates review. Pending workdays/trips and confirmed assignment days without active workdays keep costs incomplete despite reconciliation. Reports show recorded subtotal separately; total event cost and cost ratios are unavailable until complete. A preliminary report can be opened at any event lifecycle stage. Archived financial records are read-only. The review is reconciliation, not payment or a new compensation policy.

## Metrics
Active real-person registrations are distinct per event; cancelled/system-test registrations are excluded. Unique participants are the union of valid official sessions and nonvoid participant attendance, not registrations alone. Kiosk starts are distinct participants with current nonvoid official QuizAttempts, consistent with existing overview. Kiosk completions require complete status and completion timestamp. Simulator starts use recorded simulator activity starts plus accepted official results; simulator completions require current matched/resolved nonvoid official captures with matching event/session/registration/participant identities. Unresolved captures never count as accepted completions. Repeat scans/capture submissions do not add participants.

Fully completed experiences require a closed official session, no pending/in-progress/skipped recorded stages, a completed kiosk attempt and accepted simulator result. This deliberately distinguishes fully completed from the older operational session-closed count, which can include audited skips. Conversion uses completed kiosk / registrations, intersection of kiosk and simulator completers / kiosk completers, and fully completed / registrations. Drop-offs are set differences; no cause is inferred.

Kiosk score is correct answers per accepted completed attempt, reusing the existing score basis. Simulator score is existing TOTAL SCORE percent (0–100). Missing scores are unavailable. No manual lead/qualification metrics or physical participation are invented. Pending captures, inconsistent sessions, skips and unaccepted offline sync records are disclosed; no external network probes run for reporting.

Cost per registration/kiosk completion/simulator completion/full completion divides reconciled total by the corresponding distinct count. Zero denominators and incomplete cost yield unavailable. Cross-event participant totals are participant-event pairs; one person attending two events contributes to both. Weighted aggregate cost ratios use summed costs divided by summed counts, never mean event-level ratios. Any incomplete filtered event makes aggregate total and ratios unavailable. Filters use each event's local start date, status, name and existing event type. Summaries cover the full filtered set, not only a displayed page.

## Portal and permissions
Reuses Operations/internal base templates, cards, forms, scoped responsive styles, tables and six sidebar sections plus Settings gear. Event Detail, Workdays and Operations link to event reports. Operations contains Event Reports & Analytics; Admin contains Expense Categories, separate from existing Staff Compensation Settings. Existing 9D event notes are displayed only under their existing manager/assigned-staff visibility permissions. No competing notes or uploads were added.

Report access requires the existing complete event-experience permission set plus workdays.manage_workdays and event_reports.view_event_reports, preserving private participant/source compensation boundaries. Writes additionally require event_reports.manage_event_expenses. Category configuration requires event_reports.manage_expense_categories. General staff gain no financial access; migrations grant no new user permissions. Categories show usage counts without exposing financial details. Reports and exports are private/no-store.

Four CSV exports cover expense breakdown (source-labeled manual, workday pay/per diem, trips and incentive rows), distributed incentives, event performance, and filtered cross-event summary. Formula-safe values reuse 9E escaping. Exports are audited and omit participant profiles/contact information and staff identities. Category/date expense filters apply only to manual rows; overall event cost and source costs remain event-wide and are labeled accordingly.

## Validation and scope
Focused tests cover decimal/quantity validation, creation/edit/void/audits, duplicate/stale updates, category administration, historical preservation, source compensation changes, multi-day/multiple-driver trip accounting, purchase/distribution treatment, no-entry/confirmed-zero/pending/reconciliation states, registration/stage/score/skip/test/void metrics, local filters, weighted KPIs, private notes, permissions, CSRF and four exports. Final executed counts and browser/deployment evidence belong in the delivery report. A populated synthetic 9E database copy is migrated for preservation verification; original fixtures are not modified.

No payroll/payment/tax/provider integrations, new per diem/mileage policy, mapping APIs, attendance changes, customer/participant portals, BI/charting dependencies, offline synchronization or global redesign. Build 9D operational-document storage limitations are unchanged. 9G can refine organization/consistency using these report definitions without changing accounting semantics.
