- `identity.match_participant` currently returns a match for ONE email OR phone candidate; `find_or_create_participant` is called by existing staff-event and offline registration. Build 11B must not silently change these shared callers; use a separately explicit dual-contact intake resolver.
- Anonymous existing online intake creates a Participant per independent request; request keys protect retries, not person identity (`registration_views.py`).

- Existing offline reconciliation imports source identities, retains their UUID/history and creates reversible ParticipantIdentityLink records (`events/reconciliation.py`). The existing link requires an offline ParticipantMatchReview/envelope; fabricating an offline envelope for an online case is inappropriate.
- Build 11 identity read projection follows active reviewed links; cycles/conflicting links remain separate (`identity.resolved_identity_map`). New intake review links must extend this same identity facility rather than create competing canonical identities.
- No reliable field-by-field manual/review provenance exists for all legacy current-profile values. Treat legacy values conservatively as protected. New intake-owned values require explicit provenance and comparison to the last written value; ordinary staff/model updates must revoke intake ownership.
- Existing allocator concurrency race is inherited and excluded. Event registration and even the current offline path call that allocator. Atomic failure rollback/retry tests must disclose this dependency; do not repair it in Build 11B.

## Approved dual-contact resolver contract (not yet implemented)
Both validated normalized email and phone are required. Exactly one email candidate and exactly one phone candidate must be the SAME active person, with no material first/last-name or known-DOB conflict, before automatic association. Single-contact, cross-contact, multiple-record, alias ambiguity, archived identity, or name/DOB conflict enters review. Neither matching contact creates a new person. Normalization must not treat shared family contacts as proof without these safeguards.

Shared siblings/minors with the same contacts but different name/DOB therefore enter review. Changed contacts create partial/no matches rather than silently changing another identity. Multiple raw matching records still enter review; they are not silently collapsed by contact counting. A single uniquely matching reviewed alias may resolve through its existing unambiguous link to the canonical person, followed by canonical name/DOB and archive checks. Conflicting or circular links enter review. Stable UUIDs identify authorized source records, not public authentication.

Intake completion must remain possible when a relationship is uncertain. Retain raw answers, exact contract, legal acceptance evidence, source operation and original occurrence; an explicitly provisional source person may own event/consent/ticket history until staff resolves the link, without rewriting those established relationships. No account information is returned.

Concurrent submissions require source-request idempotency AND transaction-level serialization for matching/first creation; contact columns cannot simply become unique because real families may share them. PostgreSQL concurrent identical/different-person cases must be tested. Offline sync retains its source UUID and local registration/ticket relationships, re-evaluates the approved matching safeguards centrally, and remains repeat-safe/out-of-order safe.

## Material unresolved review-action decision
The existing system has no safe true established-record merge. It supports reversible links and retained source rows. The owner was asked whether **Merge & Update** may mean association of pending intake with a staff-selected existing person plus eligible unreviewed updates, while preserving all established IDs/history, or must remain pending for separately scoped true-merge design.

Owner decision received: **Use Associate & Update; defer true record merges.** The owner approved associating a reviewed intake and applying eligible answers while retaining all established participant IDs, tickets, documents and history. This resolves the affected checkpoint; implementation may proceed on that basis. A true established-record merge remains outside Build 11B. The UI must use Associate & Update, not imply a destructive merge.

Proposed other action semantics for implementation review:
- Link Only: associate reviewed intake evidence without changing protected/current profile values.
- Keep Separate: retain a distinct source person and attributable decision; do not reopen the same case automatically.
- Review Later: preserve pending intake and current relationships; no profile update or fabricated resolution.
- Associate & Update: owner-approved reviewed association plus eligible unreviewed updates, retaining established records and history.
All actions require server permissions, comparisons/conflicts, explicit confirmation, reason, revision protection and audit; no general staff permission implies DOB or private evidence access.
