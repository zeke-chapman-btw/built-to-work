Getting started

Signing in

  • Use the same sign-in page as everyone: your username or email and your password.
  • Forgot it? Use “Forgot password” on the sign-in page. The reset link goes to the email on your account.
  • Two-step sign-in (recommended): Settings → Account → Sign-in → Set up. Choose to get a 6-digit code by text, by email, or from an authenticator app, and enter it after your password. Keep your recovery codes somewhere safe.

Finding your way around

  • The menu on the left has the main areas. At the bottom: My stuff (schedule, days worked, pay), Inbox (text replies and reminders for you) and this Help page.
  • Click a card's Edit to change it in a pop-up. Bigger jobs (like a sign-up form or onboarding a customer) open as their own page.
  • All times are Eastern. A grey dash means nothing is filled in yet.

Your account

  • Settings → Account: your name, email signature, Gmail connection (so emails you send come from your own address) and password.

Working events

Your schedule

  1. My stuff → Schedule shows events you're on, with dates, arrival times, place, housing and contacts.
  2. To work an event that's open for staff, request it there. A manager approves or declines it.

On the day

  1. Check in for each day you work: pick your role (Staff, Driver, Staff Lead or Combined) and whether you drove.
  2. Mileage is worked out for you (straight-line distance from Watkinsville plus 20%, round trip).
  3. At the event you can see registrations and participants, look up phone numbers and re-send someone's ticket.

Your pay

  • If My pay is turned on for you: My stuff → My pay shows each week's pay stub and your year-to-date totals (day pay, per diem, mileage, bonuses).
  • Missing a day? Tell your manager; they can add it before payroll is approved. You'll get an email when a new pay statement is ready.

SOPs

  • SOPs in the menu has the how-to guides for events and stations.

Texts from the app

  • Two days before and the evening before an event you're working, you'll get a reminder text. If an event's date, time or place changes, you'll get a text too.

Participants & recruiting

Finding people

  • Participants → search by name, phone, email or skills. Open a profile to see everything about them: work preferences, results from the trailer, touchpoints and documents.

Reaching people

  1. On a profile (or Home → + Email/Text) press Text or Email. Pick a saved message template if one fits.
  2. For texts, choose who replies go to. Replies show up in that person's Inbox → Mine and are added to the participant's touchpoints.
  3. People who reply STOP can't be texted until they reply START.

Touchpoints & follow-ups

  • Log calls, notes and meetings on the profile's Touchpoints. Set a follow-up date and you'll get a reminder that morning.

Positions & candidates

  1. Recruiting shows each position and its candidates. Move people through the stages as you work them.
  2. When you share a candidate with a customer, it counts toward what the customer paid for (Customers → Billing shows “paid for vs delivered”).
  3. Customer responses (interested, interview request, hired) show on the candidate. You'll get a reminder until someone logs a note or call.

Managers & admins

Adding a staff member

  1. Settings → Staff → Add staff. Fill in their details, choose Create a sign-in, tick what they can do (Manager, Recruiter, Event staff…), and whether they can see their own pay.
  2. Tick any Reminder roles they cover (like Recruiting lead) so the right reminders go to them.
  3. Tick “Email them a link to set their password”. They choose their own password; nobody sends passwords.
  4. Ask them to turn on two-step sign-in and read this page.

New customers

  1. Customers → + Onboard customer. Fill it in yourself or press Send link so the customer does it: company, contacts, hiring needs, W-9 and the signed service agreement.
  2. On the Access & billing step set portal seats, the account manager and the monthly fee and dates.
  3. On Review press Create customer. Everything is set up at once and the bank setup link can go out.

Approving positions & billing

  • Only Admins and Managers can approve a position. The Approve screen sets the fee (skilled sets of 3, general labor per person, or no charge) and the customer's bank is charged right after.
  • Each customer's Billing tab shows the bank account, monthly agreements (Renew / Cancel), charges and candidates paid for vs delivered. Documents holds W-9s, insurance certificates and contracts.
  • Monthly agreements never renew on their own; the customer and account manager are reminded before the end date.

Reminders

  • Settings → Reminders lists every reminder. Change timing, who gets it and Bell/Email/Text, or add your own with + New reminder. Roles (like Recruiting lead) decide who gets most of them.

Reports

  • Reports & Analytics → Business: money collected, monthly recurring, customers paid-for vs delivered, the participant path and event costs.
