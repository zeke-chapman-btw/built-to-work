Needs Attention

About checks

These Home checks cover your accessible overdue tasks and, when authorized, the current intake-review queue and scheduled-end follow-up. They do not certify business or event readiness.

Overdue means open, non-archived work due before the current local date. It may be assigned to someone else. Intake cases include open reviews and resolved or rejected reviews with held registrations; deferred cases stay in their existing queue filter. Tasks and cases are separate units.

Scheduled-end follow-up uses the existing rule: a published event is past its scheduled end and has not been marked ended. Only events in your scope with your existing end-action access are counted. Events are a separate unit; this is not an automatic end action or readiness assessment.
