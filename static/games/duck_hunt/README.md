# Duck Hunt production assets

marsh.png is an intentional locally served production scene, not runtime/test-upload media. Generated for this project with OpenAI image generation; no external runtime dependency. It is 1672x941 and scaled to the 1920x1080 logical canvas. Ducks, reticle and effects are drawn locally by scene.js/CSS; approved BTW fonts are reused from static/kiosk/fonts.

Generation prompt:

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: offline arcade hunting game background, 1920x1080 landscape composition. Create a realistic first-person view from the shore of a southern American freshwater marsh lake. Calm muted blue green water with subtle ripples, distant natural tree line with atmospheric depth, pale blue cloudy late afternoon sky across the top 55 percent, golden warm light restrained not a dramatic sunset. Foreground reeds and marsh grasses frame the bottom edge and bottom corners only. Horizon at about 55 percent down. The central sky and water must remain clear and low contrast so animated mallard targets and readable HUD can be overlaid. Credible photographic texture, lush natural wetland detail, cinematic but natural, no oversaturated cartoon style. No ducks, no birds, no people, no guns, no text, no logos, no UI, no targets, no crosshairs. Edge-to-edge scenery only.
